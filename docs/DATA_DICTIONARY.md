## Field conventions

- Collection `score_set` is the within-set grouping boundary; `protein_group`
  joins position/reference AA/alternate AA. `legacy_unique_keys` preserves the
  historical key denominator. `*_group_state` fields describe qualification
  tiers. Distinct source values are diagnostic, not proof of independent assays.
- BRCA2 `group_id`, `protein_pos`, `n_variants` identify groups. `primary_d_*_z`
  are the signed contrasts of the same selected nucleotide keys. The primary
  concordance indicator requires both axes nonzero and of the same sign.
  Group covariance and average variance are in `holdout_paired_groups.csv`;
  Phi is `1 - sum(cov)/sum(mean_var)`, allowing negative covariance contributions.
- MSH2 `held_rep` is the evaluated culture replicate and `source_reps` the other
  two replicates. `endpoint` is never inferred from filenames. MSE metrics give
  equal weight to AA groups and equal within-group weight to DNA variants.
  `relative_rmse_gain = 1 - sqrt(mean(mse_full)/mean(mse_aa))`.
- `primary_group_errors.csv.gz` contains author-calculated group errors and
  source residual moments, not the original GEO count table. The per-variant
  prediction table is regenerated by the raw-count analysis and is not bundled.
- Missing values in coverage tables are not zero biological effects. Splicing
  pair membership was fixed before checking score availability.

Raw source hashes are in `data/external/sources.csv`. Package hashes are in
`SHA256SUMS.txt`. Portable provenance-path substitutions are recorded separately
in `docs/path_portability_changes.json`; numeric result fields are preserved.
